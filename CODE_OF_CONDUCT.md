# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not about the
person writing it.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What that looks like in practice

Behaviour that makes this project better: giving feedback on the change rather
than the contributor; accepting feedback gracefully when it's your change under
review; saying "I don't know" rather than guessing confidently; explaining *why*
you disagree instead of just blocking; and remembering that the person on the
other end may be working in a second language, a different timezone, or under a
deadline you can't see.

Behaviour that isn't acceptable: sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome
sexual attention; trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks; public or
private harassment; publishing others' private information without permission;
and persistently re-litigating a decision that has been made and documented.

Reviews on this project are deliberately direct — two AI reviewers and a human
will tell you plainly what's wrong with a change. Directness about code is
expected and welcome. Directness about people is not.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and code comments — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers via
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Shai-Alit/sas-py-vscode/security/advisories/new)
if it involves sensitive detail, or by opening an issue if it doesn't. Reports
are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is
respected.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests
that violate this document, and will explain the reason when they do. Serious or
repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from participation.
